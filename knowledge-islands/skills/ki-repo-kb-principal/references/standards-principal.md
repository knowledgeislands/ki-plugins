# Principal Knowledge Base standard

This overlay provides a visible local governance system for a Knowledge Base that a repository has already designated as principal. It composes the normal Knowledge Islands zone model and Decision Records. It does not define, select, or prove canonical-island identity, community role, or cross-island authority.

## Required principal surface

- `Admin/MEMORY.md` is the root memory anchor.
- `Admin/Governance/Charter.md` states the base's scope, stewardship, and authority boundary.
- `Admin/Governance/Known Lands.md` records the other islands and their relationship to this principal.
- `Admin/Governance/Conventions/Conventions.md` points to the local authoring and classification conventions.
- `Admin/Operations/Processes/Enactment Process.md` defines the route from Stream proposal to canonical content.

The repository's always-loaded orientation anchors the Enactment gate: substantive changes to `Admin/`, `Pillars/`, and `Resources/` proceed through an approved Stream proposal. Calendar entries and inbound triage remain lightweight exceptions.

## Boundary

The contract does not prescribe an island's name, language, integrations, community roles, pillar taxonomy, or external-source classification. Those are locally governed content. A bootstrap preserves existing material and records a proposal to migrate it; it never silently relocates content into a canonical zone.
